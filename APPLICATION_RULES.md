# Application rules

## I will not submit as you

ATS forms, identity checks, work-authorization boxes, and “I certify this is true” clicks have to be yours. I prepare the packet. You press send.

| Security+ | Yes, claimed on Credly |

## Voice

Short. Specific. No “synergistic.” Grateful without groveling. Background is nontraditional and that sentence can stay in the letter.
